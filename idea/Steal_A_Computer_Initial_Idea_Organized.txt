STEAL A COMPUTER!
Initial Game Concept — Organized Draft
Status: Early idea / incomplete; not yet brainstormed with the team

============================================================
1. CORE CONCEPT
============================================================

Steal A Computer! is a Roblox game about building computers using
real-world-inspired PC components that players choose and assemble
themselves.

Each player has a personal office in a city-themed world. The computers
they build and the hardware they collect are central to their progress,
income, hacking abilities, and cybersecurity defenses.

The intended experience combines:
- PC building and hardware choices
- Progression through earning and spending money
- Crypto mining
- Hacking and defending against other players
- Strategic choices without making the game overly complicated


============================================================
2. WORLD AND PLAYER OFFICES
============================================================

- The game world has a city theme.
- Each player has their own area designed to look like an office.
- Players' offices are arranged in a circle around the center of the map.
- The central area contains:
  - A computer-component store
  - A bank
  - Space for occasional NPC merchants to appear

The office is the player's personal space for their computers, mining
equipment, and security setup.


============================================================
3. BUYING AND COLLECTING PC COMPONENTS
============================================================

Players buy components from a computer-component store in the center
of the city.

Stock system:
- Some components are commonly available.
- Rare, powerful, or uncommon components appear less frequently and have
  limited stock.
- GPUs are intended to be especially rare compared with other components.

An occasional NPC merchant may also appear in the central area and offer:
- Rare GPUs with limited stock
- USB drives containing hacking skills or abilities


============================================================
4. EARNING MONEY: CRYPTO MINING
============================================================

Players earn in-game crypto by mining with GPUs.

- A better or rarer GPU generates more crypto.
- Players can place multiple GPUs in their office for mining, including
  GPUs mounted in racks rather than installed only in their main PC.
- Additional mining GPUs allow players to increase their income.

Converting crypto into cash:
1. The player initiates the exchange on their computer.
2. The exchanged amount becomes available to claim at the bank in the
   center of the map.

This creates a choice for players: keep crypto available in their
computer or exchange it for cash. The idea is that unexchanged crypto
could be targeted by hackers.


============================================================
5. HACKING OTHER PLAYERS
============================================================

Players can attempt to hack other players' computers.

A successful hack may allow a player to:
- Unlock another player's office
- Steal PC components, especially GPUs
- Steal the target player's current, unexchanged crypto balance

The hacking system is intended to make the computers players build
matter beyond mining and upgrades. Hardware affects both a player's
offensive hacking capabilities and their ability to defend their office.


============================================================
6. CYBERSECURITY AND COMPUTER HARDWARE
============================================================

A player's hacking and defense capabilities are influenced by the
components in their computer.

CPU:
- Affects hacking speed and defense speed.
- Influences overall hacking capabilities and available defensive
  capabilities.

RAM:
- RAM speed affects how quickly hacking actions can be performed.
- RAM capacity determines how many hacking or defensive features can
  be handled at the same time.
- RAM is envisioned as the computer's active working space or
  "inventory" during a hack or defense.

SSD:
- SSD storage capacity determines how many hacking and defensive
  features can be installed.
- RAM capacity limits how many of those installed features can be used
  at once.
- This creates a strategic choice: players must decide which features
  to install and which to use together.

Other PC components may also influence hacking or defense, but their
specific roles have not yet been decided.


============================================================
7. HACKING AND DEFENSE FEATURES
============================================================

- Players can buy hacking and defensive features through a web interface
  on their in-game computer.
- These features are purchased using in-game crypto.
- Features have different strengths and weaknesses.
- Players choose features based on their preferred hacking strategy and
  the target or situation they want to handle.
- The goal is for hardware and feature selection to create strategy,
  rather than making the game depend only on who owns the most expensive
  components.

An occasional NPC hacker may sell USB drives containing hacking skills
or abilities.


============================================================
8. INTENDED GAME FEEL
============================================================

The game should combine strategy with accessible progression.

- It does not need to be extremely complex or overly strategic.
- Players should be able to feel a steady sense of progression, similar
  to the satisfying progression found in Roblox games.
- Hardware choices and hacking/defense features should add meaningful
  decisions without overwhelming players.


============================================================
9. CURRENTLY ENVISIONED PLAYER LOOP
============================================================

1. Buy PC components.
2. Assemble or upgrade a computer.
3. Use GPUs to mine crypto.
4. Exchange crypto for cash through the in-game computer.
5. Claim the cash at the central bank.
6. Spend money on better or rarer components.
7. Install hacking or defensive features using crypto.
8. Improve the office, mining setup, hacking capabilities, or defenses.
9. Attempt to hack other players or defend against their attempts.
10. Continue progressing through hardware upgrades and competition.

This is an organized interpretation of the current concept, not a
finalized gameplay sequence. The exact order and details remain open.


============================================================
10. OPEN QUESTIONS FOR FUTURE BRAINSTORMING
============================================================

These are unresolved and have not yet been decided:

- How realistic should PC compatibility and assembly be?
- Which components, beyond CPU, RAM, SSD, and GPU, will affect gameplay?
- How exactly will hacking attempts and defenses work?
- What can a successful attacker steal, and how much?
- How can players recover after being hacked?
- How long should mining and progression take?
- How will players exchange crypto for cash, and what risks apply before
  the cash is claimed?
- How will rare component stock and NPC merchant appearances work?
- What will the first playable version include?
- Which ideas will the team keep, change, or remove?

These questions are included to make future brainstorming easier. They
are not decisions or requirements.


============================================================
11. CURRENT STATUS
============================================================

This document organizes the initial idea only. The game concept is
incomplete and has not yet been brainstormed with the other team
members. The details above describe the current vision and should be
treated as tentative.

The next step is to discuss the concept with the team, compare ideas,
and decide what belongs in the first small playable prototype.
